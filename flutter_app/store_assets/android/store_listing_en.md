## Full Description (4000 characters max)
📖 Graph Bible visualizes the relationships between biblical characters through interactive graphs, helping you understand the Bible in a new way.

✨ Key Features:

🔗 Character Relationship Graph
• Data on over 600 biblical characters
• More than 350 relationships (family, spiritual, conflicts, etc.)
• Drag nodes to explore connections
• Pinch to zoom in/out
• Highlight related relationships when selecting a character

📅 Timeline
• Major events organized by era
• From Creation to the Early Church
• Understand the temporal context of characters and events

📚 Bible Reading
• Complete 66 books of the Bible
• 39 Old Testament, 27 New Testament books
• Navigate by chapter and verse
• Clean dark theme UI

🔍 Search Function
• Search by character name, role, or description
• Quick filters (Prophets, Kings, Disciples, etc.)
• View character details directly from search results

🌐 Bilingual Support
• Switch between Korean and English
• All character information available in both languages

💡 Graph Bible is perfect for:
• Those who want to study the Bible systematically
• Those curious about biblical character relationships
• Those who want to understand Bible timelines
• Those who want to explore the Bible in a new way

📱 Features:
• Works offline (no internet required)
• Dark theme reduces eye strain
• Intuitive touch interface
• Fast app launch

Explore the world of the Bible through graphs!
